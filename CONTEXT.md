# Partnership Intelligence

Partnership Intelligence helps an organization identify and pursue partnerships using its needs, existing relationships, and evidence about potential partners.

## Language

**Organization**:
The nonprofit, student organization, NGO, club, or similar group seeking partnerships through its own installation.
_Avoid_: Customer organization, tenant when describing the product domain.

**Company**:
A potential or existing external partner. A company record may describe a business, foundation, university, or other institution.
_Avoid_: Sponsor when no sponsorship exists.

**Need**:
A current requirement that an organization hopes to meet through a partnership. Needs may involve money, goods, services, facilities, expertise, or people.

**Member**:
A person who currently belongs to the organization.

**Alumni**:
People who previously belonged to the organization.

**Advisor**:
A person who advises or supports the organization through an ongoing advisory relationship.

**Contact**:
A known person at an external partner who may be relevant to a partnership. A suggested job role without a named person is a recommended contact role.
_Avoid_: Decision-maker unless decision authority is supported by evidence.

**Relationship**:
A recorded connection between people or organizations, such as employment, a personal acquaintance, or an introduction. A recorded connection does not by itself establish willingness to provide an introduction.

**Relationship path**:
A sequence of recorded connections linking the organization to a potential partner or contact.
_Avoid_: Warm introduction when the introduction has not happened.

**Partnership**:
An agreed collaboration between the organization and an external partner, including sponsorship, supply, services, or technical collaboration.
_Avoid_: Opportunity when an agreement already exists.

**Opportunity**:
A proposed partnership that connects a particular organizational need to a potential partner, with supporting evidence and a recommended next action.

**Outreach**:
A recorded attempt to approach a potential partner or arrange an introduction. A draft message alone is not completed outreach.

**Outcome**:
A recorded result of pursuing an opportunity, such as a declined request, an agreed partnership, or an unresolved conversation.

**Evidence**:
A source or attributed observation supporting a specific claim. Evidence records preserve where the claim came from and when it was observed.

**Inference**:
A conclusion drawn from evidence that has not itself been established as a fact.

**Priority**:
A transparent ordering of opportunities using explicit factors. Priority is not a probability of securing a partnership.
