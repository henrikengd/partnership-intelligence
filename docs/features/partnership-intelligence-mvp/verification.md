## Scope and limits

MIT source is organization-agnostic. Real deployments, personal records, credentials, backups and screenshots stay outside public source. No automatic scraping/discovery, autonomous sending, inbox integration, graph/vector service, durable job queue or multi-organization/per-record permission platform is included. One app process serves an installation. Structural AI checks do not prove semantic support, pseudonyms do not guarantee anonymity, and deletion cannot erase separately retained copies.

Public DNS/certificates, paid provider behavior, production deployment and merging are outside this delivery proof. The feature PR remains unmerged. Runtime and review proof binds the source above; current remote CI and final tracker state are verified separately during publication.
